# Publishing checklist (Medium)
1. New story on Medium. Paste the title and subtitle from the top of article.md.
2. Paste the body section by section. Headings: use Medium's big/small title styles.
3. Upload each image from article/images/ where the `![...]` line sits (GIFs: 02, 03, 07 stay animated).
   - Cover/top image: 01_poster.png (Medium crops to wide; consider using 02b_pipeline_still.png as the cover instead and the poster as the first inline image).
4. Gists: on an empty line paste the gist URL and press Enter, Medium embeds it. The three URLs are in the "Reproduce it" section.
5. Repo link needs the public repo https://github.com/Majid460/avif-shootout to exist and be pushed BEFORE you publish.
6. Tags: rust, android, performance, c, benchmarking.
7. Re-read the "What I did not measure" paragraph and the fuzzing caveat; keep them.
